Act I, The Weeping Woods · first page of a commissioned game script Rain. The steady percussion of it against old glass.

You don’t remember falling asleep in the university library. You remember the smell of aged parchment and cold coffee, the amber halo of a single desk lamp, a stack of texts you told yourself you’d read by morning. You remember reaching for the top shelf, fingertips grazing a spine you didn’t recognize, and then warmth. Sudden, violent warmth, as if the book itself had a pulse.

The world came apart.
